About Me

Raised in Dehradun at the threshold of the Garhwal Himalayas, I developed a passion for engineering driven by local reality. In hilly regions and smaller towns, expensive GPUs and ultra-high-speed fiber networks aren't guaranteed.

This fueled my obsession with Edge AI and Serverless Cloud Architecture. Through EDP and Entrepreneurship Bootcamps at IIT Roorkee TIDES and IIM Kashipur FIED, I refined Newlife Tech—a real-time Indian Sign Language (ISL) interpreter. I refused to build another bloated cloud API that costs dollars per minute. Instead, I architected lightweight 21-landmark coordinate extraction running in-browser on client devices at 60 FPS, turning standard smartphone webcams into live translation bridges.

Beyond coding, I live by the scout motto "Be Prepared" earned as a Nipun Rover in The Bharat Scouts and Guides, guiding every decision. Today, as the AWS Student Builder Group Leader at Govt Degree College Dehradun Shahar, I mentor fellow undergraduates on cloud fundamentals, guiding 300+ students toward their AWS Builder IDs and launching their first serverless cloud deployments.

Certifications & Validations: Certified by NASSCOM, Boot.dev, Infosys Springboard, and Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) in Prompt Engineering, Backend Systems, Cybersecurity, AI, and Office Tools.
